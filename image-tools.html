Privacy Policy

Image Tools · studio.nexor.image_tools
Last updated: 5 August 2026

The short version. Image Tools does its work entirely on your phone. The photos you choose using your device’s photo picker are never uploaded, never sent to us, and never leave your device. We do not operate a server, we have no account system, and we cannot see your files. The app shows ads, and the advertising provider collects data described below.

Who we are

Image Tools is published by Nexor Studio (“we”, “us”). The app lets you compress, resize, crop, rotate and convert photos, and combine them into PDFs.

Files you open in the app

We collect none of them. All processing happens locally on your device using software bundled inside the app. Your files are not transmitted anywhere, and no copy is retained by us.

Files the app produces are saved into the app’s own private storage area on your device. Other apps cannot read that area. Those files stay there until you delete them, share them somewhere yourself, or uninstall the app — uninstalling deletes them permanently.

Permissions

Image Tools requests no runtime permissions at all — no camera permission, no storage permission, no photo-library permission, no location permission.

Where the app needs a file or an image, it hands you over to a picker provided by Android or Google Play services. You choose what to share, and only the item you picked is passed back to the app. The app never gains the ability to browse your device on its own.

Advertising

The app is free and is funded by advertising through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device’s advertising identifier
  • your IP address, and an approximate location derived from it
  • device information such as model, operating-system version and language
  • whether you saw, tapped or dismissed an ad

This collection is performed by Google, not by us, and is governed by Google’s Privacy & Terms. We never receive your files, your identity, or the contents of anything you process in the app.

In regions where consent is legally required, the app shows Google’s consent form before requesting any ad, and your choice is respected. You can also limit or reset your advertising ID at any time in Settings → Google → Ads on your device.

Purchases

The optional Remove ads upgrade is processed by Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment; we never see or store your card details, billing address, or any payment information. The app stores only a single yes/no flag on your device recording whether the upgrade is active.

What we do not do

  • No user accounts, sign-ins or profiles
  • No analytics or crash-reporting service
  • No tracking of what you do inside the app
  • No selling or sharing of personal information
  • No servers of our own — there is nowhere for your data to be sent

Children

Image Tools is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Remove all app data — uninstall the app. Everything it stored on your device, including files it produced, is deleted.
  • Limit ad personalisation — reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings, or change your consent choice where the consent form is shown.
  • Remove ads entirely — buy the one-time upgrade. No ads are requested afterwards, so no further advertising data is collected.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page and, where the change is significant, note it in the app’s release notes.
